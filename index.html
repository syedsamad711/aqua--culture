01 • INTRODUCTION

What is Aquaculture?

📖 Introduction

Aquaculture means farming aquatic organisms such as fish, prawns, molluscs and aquatic plants under managed conditions.

It differs from capture fishing because aquatic organisms are stocked, fed and protected as part of a managed system.

🌱 Importance

  • Provides food and nutrition.
  • Supports livelihoods.
  • Helps in planned use of aquatic resources.
  • Encourages responsible aquatic management.

💧 Important Water Factors

Temperature Monitor regularly
pH Suitable level
Dissolved Oxygen Maintain oxygen

🐟 Fish Health

Regularly observe swimming, appetite and signs of stress.

Filtration, cleaning and aeration help maintain suitable conditions.

Regular observation helps identify problems early.

06 • FEEDING

Feeding & Nutrition

🍽️

Fish Feed

Fish may receive pellets, flakes or granules depending on species and life stage.

🦐

Shrimp Feed

Shrimp feed can contain nutrients such as protein and energy.

⚖️

Feed Management

Amount and frequency should be managed to avoid unnecessary waste.

⚠️ Uneaten feed can reduce water quality, so feeding should be managed carefully.
